Add text

Tap the + button and then Text. A text layer is added in the middle of the canvas (gray, Roboto Regular, 18 pt, left aligned) and the full-screen text editor opens at once.

At the top of the editor:

  • Alignment cycles left, center, right and justify.
  • The font name (shown in its own style) opens a strip with your recent fonts, your favorites and Show All Fonts.
  • The size button (such as "18pt") opens a slider with 25 sizes from 5 to 144 pt.
  • The color swatch opens the color picker with the eyedropper.
  • The tick (Done) closes the editor.

Under them is the preview and then the text box, which says "Enter your text here." until you type. Drag the round handle at the edge of the text frame in the preview to change the width at which lines wrap. The name of a text layer is its text, until you rename it.

To edit the text later, select the layer and tap Edit Text.

Style text

The other tools work on text as on any layer.

  • Fill & Color gives the letters a color, a gradient or a picture or video (Media).
  • Frame & Shadow: Stroke is the outline of the letters. Border and Shadow add more.
  • Opacity & Blend Mode, Transform and Effects work as for shapes.
  • Convert to Outline (layer menu) turns text into a shape you can edit point by point. It cannot be turned back into text.

Emoji follow the Emoji Style setting: your phone's emoji or the set that comes with the app. See Settings.

Fonts

The catalog has 150 font families. 50 of them are built into the app. The other 100 are downloaded from Google Fonts the first time you choose one (they show a cloud icon and "Downloads when chosen"; the download needs an internet connection). A project that uses fonts you do not have shows Fonts not on this phone when you open it, and the text uses another font until you download them.

The font browser

Show All Fonts opens a grid of fonts, each shown with a sample. At the top is a menu button that opens a drawer with these filters:

  • Categories: Serif, Sans Serif, Display, Handwriting, Monospace, Favorite and Imported.
  • Sorting: Popular or Alphabetical.
  • Languages: All Languages or one of sixteen scripts such as Arabic, Cyrillic, Greek, Hebrew, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Thai, Vietnamese and Latin.
  • Import Fonts.

The search field at the top is labeled "Type here to search". The star on a font marks it as a favorite.

Your own fonts

Import Fonts lets you pick font files from your phone. If a file cannot be used, a list says which ones failed. Press and hold an imported font to remove it.

Animate text

The effects of the Text category animate text letter by letter.

  • Text Progress reveals the text by character, word or line (typewriter and fade in by word are presets).
  • Text Transform moves, rotates, scales and fades each letter in turn (presets: Wave and Spin Cascade).
  • Text Randomizer scrambles letters before they settle (preset: Scramble).
  • Count Up/Down and Timecode turn text into a counter or a clock.

See Using effects for how to add them, and the effects library for every setting.

Captions

Auto Captions listens to the speech in a video or sound and makes one text layer per caption, on the phone and offline. See AI tools.
