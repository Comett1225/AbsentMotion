Sharpen

Stylize · Added in version 2.1.0 or earlier

Applies an unsharp mask using a 3x3 neighborhood to enhance fine details.

Sharpen brings out fine detail. Strength is how much, and Radius how wide the sharpened edges are. Too much makes halos around edges. For a different sharpening method, try Unsharp Mask.

Settings

SettingTypeRangeDefault
StrengthNumber0 to 201
RadiusNumber0.5 to 101
