Film Grain

Noise · Added in version 2.1.0 or earlier

Animated monochrome film grain with adjustable amount, grain size, and animation speed.

Film Grain adds animated grain like old film. Amount is how visible it is, Grain Size how coarse, and Speed how fast it flickers; at 0 the grain stands still.
