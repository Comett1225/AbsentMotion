Denoise

Blur · Added in version 2.5.0

Removes image noise with an edge-preserving bilateral filter that averages nearby pixels only when their colors are similar.

Denoise removes grain and noise while trying to keep edges sharp. Strength is how much noise is removed, Radius how far it looks around each pixel and Detail how much fine detail is kept. Too much makes skin and surfaces look flat.

Settings

SettingTypeRangeDefault
StrengthNumber0 to 10.5
RadiusNumber1 to 62
DetailNumber0 to 10.5
