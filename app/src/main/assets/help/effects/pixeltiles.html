Pixel Tiles

Stylize · Added in version 4.3.0

Rebuilds the layer from a grid of small pixel-pattern tiles. Each tile picks how much of its pattern to draw from the brightness under it, like a mix of ASCII art and mosaic. Chaos breaks the grid up with random tiles.
