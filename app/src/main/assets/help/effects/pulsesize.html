Pulse Size

Move · Added in version 3.0.0

Grows and shrinks the layer over and over, on a sine or triangle wave.

Pulse Size grows and shrinks a layer over and over. Shrink and Grow are the smallest and largest size as a multiple of normal (0.9 is 90 %), Frequency is the number of pulses per second and Wave is Sine (smooth) or Triangle. A heartbeat or a beat-synced logo are typical uses. See also Pulse Opacity.
