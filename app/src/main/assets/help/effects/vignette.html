Vignette

Stylize · Added in version 2.1.0 or earlier

Darkens the layer toward its edges outside a rounded shape: Size sets the shape, Roundness how round it is, Feather how soft its edge is, Strength how dark it gets and Tint how much it takes on Color. Punch Out makes the edges transparent instead.

A vignette darkens the corners of the picture to draw the eye to the middle. Size and Roundness shape the clear area, Feather softens its edge, Strength sets how dark the corners get, and Color and Tint let the edges take a colour instead of black. Punch Out makes the edges transparent instead of dark.
