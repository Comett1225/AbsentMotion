Cube

3D · Added in version 3.0.0

Wraps the layer onto every face of a turning cube with rounded edges and lighting.

Cube wraps the layer onto the faces of a 3D cube. Rotation X, Rotation Y and Rotation Z turn it (animate them for a spinning cube), Size and Scale change how big it is, Perspective the depth effect, and Shading, Light Angle and Shine the lighting. See Camera layers and 3D.
