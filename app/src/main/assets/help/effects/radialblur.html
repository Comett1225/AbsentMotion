Zoom Blur

Blur · Added in version 2.1.0 or earlier

Applies a radial zoom blur by averaging samples along rays from a center point with adjustable strength.

Zoom Blur smears the picture outward from a point, as if you were zooming the camera. Center is the point and Strength the length of the streaks. Animate Strength for a quick zoom transition.
