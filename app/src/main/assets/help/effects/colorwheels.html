Lift Gamma Gain

Color · Added in version 2.1.0 or earlier

Color wheels for the shadows (lift), midtones (gamma) and highlights (gain): a hue, how far to push it, and a level.

Lift, gamma and gain are the three tonal ranges used in colour grading: Lift is the shadows, Gamma the midtones and Gain the highlights. For each, Hue picks a colour, Amount how far to push toward it and Level how light or dark the range becomes.
