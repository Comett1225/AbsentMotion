RGB Split

Stylize · Added in version 2.1.0 or earlier

Splits the red, green and blue channels: the Center channel stays put and the other two shift in opposite directions along Angle. Light adds the shifted colors, Dark keeps them solid, Mask keeps only where all three overlap and Luma makes the fringes as opaque as they are bright.

RGB Split pulls the red, green and blue channels apart, like a lens with colour fringing. Strength is how far they move, Angle the direction. Center is the channel that stays in place and Mode decides how the shifted channels are combined: Light adds them, Dark keeps them solid, Mask keeps only where all three overlap, and Luma makes the fringes as opaque as they are bright.
