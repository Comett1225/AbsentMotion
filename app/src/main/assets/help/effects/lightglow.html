Light Glow

Stylize · Added in version 2.1.0 or earlier

Adds a colored glow around the parts of the picture brighter than a threshold.

Light Glow only glows where the picture is brighter than Threshold. Lower the threshold to make more of the picture glow, raise Intensity for a stronger glow, and change Size for how far it spreads. Color tints the glow.

Use it on night scenes, lights, neon and bright highlights. For a halo around a shape, use Glow.
