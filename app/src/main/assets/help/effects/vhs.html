VHS

Stylize · Added in version 3.0.0

Looks like an old video tape: color bleed, smeared lines, scanlines, tape noise and rolling tracking bands.

VHS makes the layer look like an old video tape. The settings control Color Bleed, Smear, Scanlines, tape Noise, rolling Tracking bands, Wobble and Saturation; Head Switching turns the tape's head-switching distortion on or off. Seed changes the random noise pattern.

Use it on a whole group to give a scene a tape look, and add Film Grain or RGB Split for more.
