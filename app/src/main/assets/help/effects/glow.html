Glow

Stylize · Added in version 2.1.0 or earlier

A halo in one color around the outside of the layer's shape. Hardness turns it from a soft fade into a solid outline.

Glow draws a halo around the outside of the layer's shape. Set Radius for how far it reaches, Color for its color and Alpha for how strong it is. Hardness changes the halo from a soft fade (low) to a solid outline (high). Blending Mode is Screen by default, which lights the area around the shape; Normal paints the color as it is.

  • Animate Radius up and down for a pulsing glow.
  • For a glow that comes from the bright parts of a picture instead of from its shape, use Light Glow or Soft Glow.
