Wipe

Key · Added in version 3.0.0

Hides the layer from two opposite sides. Animate Start or End for a wipe transition.

Wipe hides the layer from two opposite sides. Animate Start or End to reveal or remove the layer with a straight edge; Angle is the direction of the edge and Feather softens it. For a wipe like a clock hand, use Radial Wipe.
