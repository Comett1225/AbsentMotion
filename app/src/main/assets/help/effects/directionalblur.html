Directional Blur

Blur · Added in version 2.1.0 or earlier

Motion blur by averaging samples along a directional angle.

Directional Blur smears the layer in one direction. Length is how far and Angle is the direction. It is a fixed blur: for a blur that follows how the layer actually moves, use Motion Blur.
