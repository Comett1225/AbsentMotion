Adjustment Layer

Color · Added in version 2.1.0 or earlier

Copies what is drawn beneath the layer so later effects can change it. Mask To Layer limits the copy to the opaque parts of the layer. Mix brings the layer fill back. Effects Reach Outside lets later effects, such as Stretch, carry the copy past the layer's edges.

An Adjustment Layer copies everything drawn below it, so that the effects placed after it change all of those layers at once. Put the layer above the ones you want to grade, add this effect, then add effects such as Hue & Saturation or Blur after it. Mask To Layer limits the change to the layer's own shape, which lets you blur or tint only a part of the picture. Mix brings the layer's own fill back.
