Blur

Blur · Added in version 2.1.0 or earlier

A smooth, even blur.

Strength is the size of the blur: 0 is sharp, and higher numbers soften the layer more. It works on photos, video, shapes, text and groups.

  • Animate Strength from a high number down to 0 to bring a title or photo into focus.
  • To blur everything that is behind a layer, add an Adjustment Layer first and Blur after it.
  • To blur along movement, use Motion Blur. To keep the outline sharp, use Inner Blur.
