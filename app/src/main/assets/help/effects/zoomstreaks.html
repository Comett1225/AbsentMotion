Zoom Streaks

Blur · Added in version 3.0.0

Makes streaks that shoot out from a point by scaling copies of the layer around it and keeping the brightest pixels. Average gives a zoom blur. R, G, B and A choose per channel whether the copies keep the lowest, highest or average value, or leave that channel alone.
