1. ENGINE LIBRARIES
-------------------

AbsentMotion's renderer is C++ on top of Skia. Skia and the libraries it needs are built from source (see docs/BUILD.md) and linked statically into the engine library, libamengine.so. Speech recognition lives in a second library, libwhisper_jni.so, loaded only when captions are generated.

- Own drawing: made for AbsentMotion from circles, lines, rectangles and arcs; Apache-2.0 like the rest of the code.
- Effect thumbnails are not icons: they are small renders of six CC0 photographs, listed in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md (section 5, "Effect thumbnail photos").

How the Lucide shapes were used: the SVG shapes (path, circle, rect, line) were joined into one path-data string on the same 24 unit grid, written with absolute commands, and tinted and stroked by the app (round caps and joins, 1.2 to 2.5 dp strokes depending on the icon). Where the Notes column says "scaled" or "mirrored" the shape was scaled about the grid centre or flipped left to right; nothing else about the geometry was changed. "Moved" means shifted to sit inside the icon's own viewport (for example a 32 dp speaker). The path strings themselves live in the *Icons.kt files.

The launcher icon is an own drawing, made by tools/icon/make_icon.py (source of the mark: tools/icon/mark.svg).
