First public release. AbsentMotion is a free, open-source motion graphics editor for Android: layers, keyframes with easing, shapes, text with Google Fonts, effects, camera layers, audio, offline text to speech, on-device auto captions, MP4 and GIF export. Opens and exports Alight Motion scene XML. Needs Android 14, a 64-bit ARM phone and Vulkan 1.2. No account, no ads, no subscription.
