AbsentMotion is a free, open-source motion graphics and animation editor. There is no subscription, no account and no advertising. Your projects stay on your device.

What you can do

- Build scenes from layers on a timeline: shapes, text, images, video, audio, freehand drawings, groups and reusable elements.
- Animate any value with keyframes and easing curves (cubic Bezier, bounce, elastic, cyclic, random and steps), edited on a graph.
- Draw vector shapes and edit their points, or draw freehand with pen, brush, fill and eraser.
- Set text in any font from Google Fonts. Fonts are downloaded when you pick them.
- Add borders, shadows, gradients, blend modes, clipping masks and per-layer effects.
- Use camera layers with perspective, depth, focus blur, fog and motion blur.
- Mix audio, mark the beat of a song with bookmarks, and turn text into speech with the voices on your device, offline.
- Generate captions from speech, on the device. The speech model is downloaded the first time you use the feature, after the app shows its size.
- Export MP4 video, animated GIF, image sequences and single frames.

AbsentMotion can open scene XML files and .amproj packages exported from Alight Motion, and it exports scene XML in the same format. Effects that AbsentMotion does not include are kept in the file and are not drawn. AbsentMotion saves its own packages as .absproj. AbsentMotion is an independent project. Good to know

- The set of built-in effects is small at first.
- Requires Android 14 or newer and a 64-bit ARM device with Vulkan 1.1.
- This build contains no Google libraries, so there is no background removal.
