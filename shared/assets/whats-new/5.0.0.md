# What's new in 5.0

The biggest update yet: **AbsentMotion on your computer**, **real 3D** with **lights**, **colour palettes**, **Adobe Animate import** and **eight new languages**. AbsentMotion isn't perfect yet, so if something breaks or doesn't look right, please tell us through [GitHub Issues](github-issues), our [Google Form](bug-form) or [Discord](discord)!

## New features
- **AbsentMotion on your computer**. Meaning **WINDOWS**, **MAC** and **LINUX** users! Remember, AbsentMotion is not competing with Davinci or Adobe AE, it's only an alternative to having something close to AlightMotion on PC!
- **Chromebooks with Intel or AMD chips** - get their **own version**, and the in-app update picks it.
- **Depth order** - with a camera, 3D layers sit **in front of and behind each other**. The camera can **tilt** and **orbit**.
- **Light layers** - **Ambient**, **Directional** and **Point** lights shade 3D models, 3D Scatter and 3D Fracture.
- **Sort by piece** - shards, scatter pieces, model faces and particles sort **one by one**, so objects fly through each other.
- **Colour palettes** - in every colour picker: make one from a **picture** or a **palette file**, then sort, lock and export it.
- **Adobe Animate import** - open **.fla files** from Flash CS5 on. Shapes, symbols, tweens, masks and sounds come over.
- **Text Shake** - letters **shake on their own**: Shake, Jitter, Wave, Circle or Wobble, or only some letters at a time.
- **Text Blips** - types the text out with a **blip for each letter**. Eight voices, heard in the preview and the export.
- **ASCII** - turns any layer into **text art** made of letters and symbols, with six presets.
- **Edit Group** - **open a group** to change the layers inside; the top bar leads back. **Ungroup** puts the layers back.
- **Group Re-timing** - the gear of an open group can **Freeze**, **Stretch**, **Loop** or **Repeat & Stretch** it.
- **Trim, Extract Audio, Make a GIF** - three new tools in the **Tools** tab.
- **Tablet layouts** - big tablets get panels **beside the preview** and a taller timeline, in portrait and landscape.
- **Preview renders ahead** - while you pause, frames around the playhead get ready. A **thin green line** on the ruler shows them.
- **Eight new languages** - **Korean**, **Chinese** (Traditional and Simplified), **French**, **German**, **Italian**, **Polish** and **Ukrainian**, help pages included.

## Desktop
- **Mac** - runs on Macs with **Apple silicon** and on Macs with **Intel** chips.
- **Windows** - an installer with **Start menu** and **desktop shortcuts**.
- **Linux** - for **arm64** and **x86_64**, as a **.deb**, an **AppImage** or a **.tar.gz** with an install script.
- **Made for mouse and keyboard** - a **media library**, an **inspector**, **right-click menus** and **keyboard shortcuts**.
- **Window bar** - in the app's colours, with the system's window buttons and a **File, Edit, View, Playback, Help** menu.
- **Mouse timeline** - drag clips to move, trim and reorder them with **snapping**, and select with a click, Shift or a box.
- **Shortcuts** - **Space** plays, **Alt+Left** and **Alt+Right** jump between bookmarks, **comma** and **period** nudge the selected clips.
- **Drag and drop** - drop **media** or **palette files** onto the window.
- **Media per project** - each project keeps its own media and **linked folders**. **Pin to all projects** shows a folder everywhere.
- **Level meter** - one meter for the whole project. With nothing selected, the **Inspector** shows the project and its layers.
- **Help window** - effect help opens in **its own window** inside the app.
- **Export in the background** - keep working while a video exports.
- **The phone's tools too** - AI captions, background removal, noise reduction, Text to Speech, 3D models, motion tracking and the **webcam**.
- **Open with** - project files open from Finder, Explorer or your file manager.
- **In-app updates** - new versions are offered **inside the app**.

## More new features
- **Repeat per copy** - Linear, Radial, Grid and Along Path repeats: a **delay per copy**, and **random** position, rotation, scale and opacity.
- **Font Weight** - animate how **thick the letters** are, with variable fonts (imported ones too).
- **Parenting Helper** - lock or weigh how a child follows its parent's **scale and rotation**, or roll it like a wheel.
- **Scale Assist** - extra scale on X, Y or both, or a **squash and stretch**.
- **Alight Motion imports** - Parenting Helper, Scale Assist and the **3D orientation** of layers now come over.
- **Alight Motion presets** - keep **more effect settings**. Edge Glow, Fractal Warp, Chroma Key, Soft Glow and Electric Edges look closer.
- **Deep Glow, HSL and Drop Shadow** - three new effects.
- **3D layer** - a tile that lets a **flat layer** join the depth order.
- **Stretch Timing** - make selected clips **longer or shorter together**. Animations stretch, and videos play slower or faster to fit.
- **Several clips at once** - stretch the ends of all selected clips together, and **split** them all at the playhead.
- **Clipping arrow** - a clipped layer shows a small **hooked arrow** pointing down to the layer it is clipped to.
- **Effect swipes** - swipe an effect **left to delete** it, and swipe inside a category for the next or previous one.
- **Recent and Favorite** - swipe the effects library grid, or hold the two small icons and slide, to switch.
- **Puppet Pin** - smooth curling, **Position, Bend and Stiff** pins, mesh settings and **Sway**.
- **Preset pictures** - the **Presets** button on an open effect shows every preset with a picture.
- **Favourite presets** - star a preset and it shows **first** in the Favorite strip.
- **Mark Beats tile** - audio layers have **Mark Beats** right in their tools.
- **90 and 120 fps** - projects can now run at **90** or **120** frames per second.
- **Saved sizes** - keep your **custom sizes** in New Project and in the project settings.
- **Copy and Report** - crash and error screens can **copy** the details or **report** them on [GitHub](github-issues), [Discord](discord) or the [bug form](bug-form).
- **Stereo levels** - the level line under the play bar shows **left and right**. Sideways tablets show a level meter too.

## Improvements
- **Copy Background** - the **Adjustment Layer** has a new name and an **Other** category. Searching "adjustment layer" still finds it.
- **Effects library** - presets left the grid: **search a preset's name** to find its effect. **New** lists the 5.x effects.
- **Gradient dots** - while you edit a gradient, dragging on the canvas moves the **nearest dot**, not the layer.
- **Effect grip** - a **short tap** opens the effect; **hold** the grip to move it.
- **Effect cards** - an opening card **moves to the top** and the others slide away. Closing puts it back.
- **Effect headers** - effect and border headers are **lighter**.
- **Electric Edges** - works like Alight Motion's: **Thickness** makes a glowing band and a keyframed **Seed** crackles. Older projects keep their look.
- **Safer start** - if the app closes while the graphics start, the **next start uses the other one** (OpenGL or Vulkan).
- **Older Mali and PowerVR phones** - start with **OpenGL**, which is safer on them.
- **Samsung updates** - if **Auto Blocker** may be stopping an update, the app says so and how to get past it.
- **Help on tablets** - opens in a **wide panel** with the pictures beside the text.
- **Tablet home and tools** - projects, templates and tools show as a **grid**. Tool screens get **two columns** sideways.
- **Small tablets** - sheets such as the colour picker open as a **card in the middle**.
- **Chromebooks** - the app first opens in a **large window**.
- **No more Play build** - Play installs move to the **foss build** when they update, and keep their projects and settings.
- **New licence** - AbsentMotion 5.0 and later are **GPL-3.0**. 4.4 and earlier stay Apache-2.0.

## Performance
- **Editing with videos** - phones and tablets no longer wait for the video on each step. The exact frame follows.
- **After a restart** - effects **stutter less** the first time, because phones and tablets keep their prepared graphics.
- **Prepared frames** - a change to one layer only throws away the frames of that layer and the layers tied to it.
- **Glow** - layers with Glow and other effects after it draw **faster**.
- **Text** - animated text draws **faster**, and projects with many fonts use less memory.

## Bug fixes
- **Canvas edge** - no more thin light line along the edge of the canvas.
- **Puppet Pin** - no longer cuts off parts pulled in from **outside the canvas**, also with Glow.
- **Full Blur preview** - no longer risks freezing weaker tablets.
- **Parenting** - linking or unlinking a parent no longer **adds keyframes**. The layer keeps its animation and stays in place.
- **Edit Points** - dragging a **rounded corner**, such as the House roof, moves the point instead of bending the curve.
- **Keyframe diamonds** - show at the **right times** after the project's frame rate changes.
- **Smooth** - smoothing a trimmed clip with speed keyframes no longer changes its **timing**.
- **Reduce Noise** - no longer moves the sound **earlier** when a file's sound starts late or has gaps.
- **Start the tutorial again** - no longer leaves an **extra practice project** behind each time.
- **Favorite strip** - scrolls to **every favourite** effect, not only the first ten.
- **Chromebooks** - Home, Settings, Export and About no longer sit **under the window's title bar**.
