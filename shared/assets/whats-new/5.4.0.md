# What's new in 5.4.0

**Five new effects**, the **Toji Motion** pack with **143 effects** to download, **installed fonts** in the font list, **speed** for text, null and group layers, a **new Settings page**, **Create theme**, and clips whose files are gone now **show it**.

## New features

### Effects
- **Stabilizer** - steadies a **shaky video**: pick **Low**, **Medium** or **High**, the clip is measured once and the picture moves against the shake.
- **Warm Glow** - a soft blur that keeps the **darker** colors, with **Level** and a **Mask** (Rectangle, Ellipse or Polygon).
- **Broken Glass** - shatters the picture into **shards** with cracks from the impact point; **Debris** sets how many.
- **Vibrato** and **Ring Modulator** - two new **audio effects**: a wavering pitch, and a metallic, robot-like sound.
- **Toji Motion pack** - **143 effects** by the modders **Toji Motion** and **Tanryu**, at the top of **More Effects** and in the **Community**. It downloads only when you tap **Add**, opens to **categories** like the Effects Library, and offers an **Update** when new effects are added.

### Editor
- **Speed for text, null and group layers** - like in Alight Motion: the layer gets shorter or longer and its animation plays at the new speed.
- **Missing clips** - when a project uses files that were **moved or deleted**, it tells you when it opens, and those clips turn **grey with a Ø** on the timeline.
- **Installed fonts** - fonts **installed on your computer or phone** now show up in the font list on their own, right under your imported fonts, with every weight. No need to import them one by one.
- **Auto Captions: Select different source** - pick the clips to caption **right in the editor**: clips without sound are greyed out, tap one or hold several and tap **Done**.

### Settings
- **New Settings page** - tiles for each part of the app, with the less used settings under **Advanced**. The **slide-out menu** keeps the important ones, and a **gear** on Home opens the page.
- **Create theme** - make your own theme **color by color**, starting from any built-in theme or an **imported theme file**, then save or export it. A new **Themes** help page lists every color.
- **Selection colors** - themes can set the **selected clip's border** and **trim handles** on the computer timeline (**SelectionBorder**, **SelectionHandle**).

## Improvements

### Effects
- **More Effects** - the **Added** category is now **More Effects**. Folders with many effects open to **categories** first.
- **Box** and **Flip** look **the same** as in Alight Motion.

### Editor
- **Smoother text editor** on phones: no jumping when it opens or closes, and the **✓** first closes an open color, font or size panel.
- **Changelog button** next to the menu button on Home.
- **Clip names** on the computer timeline are **bolder**.

### Updates and data
- **Lighter update checks** - the app asks the server only whether something changed, so a check with nothing new costs **almost no data**.
- **Clearer crash reports** - a crash in the video engine now lists **the part that crashed first**.

## Bug fixes
- **SVG layers** - an imported SVG made **longer** on the timeline no longer disappears after its first 2 seconds.
- **Remove Background on phone videos** - clips saved **sideways** (most portrait phone videos) are cut out properly.
- **Tile and Tiles** - no more see-through gaps or **dark lines** between tiles.
- **Eyedropper** - Chroma Key and other effect colors pick the **layer's own color**, not the clip behind it.
- **"Apply to children"** - the right words in French, Spanish, Portuguese, Persian, Filipino, Indonesian and Malay.
