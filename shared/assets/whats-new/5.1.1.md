# What's new in 5.1.1

**Named custom sizes**, **Use anyway** for heavy features, **snapping on the preview** and a fix for **Motion Blur** cutting layers off.

## New features
- **Named custom sizes** - save **as many sizes as you like**, each with a **name**, on a second page of the size row in **New project** (swipe left). The tiles take the **size's shape**, long names scroll, and **holding a tile** offers Rename and Delete.
- **Use anyway** - features your phone may struggle with (**Optical flow High**, **AI Smooth**, **AI Enhance**, **AI background removal 2.0** and export sizes your phone's encoder does not list) show a **warning icon** instead of being locked, and **ask before each use**.
- **Snapping on the preview** - dragging a layer on the preview **holds at the centre lines** and at other layers' centres and edges, with guide lines and a small vibration, just like the **Move** pad.

## Improvements
- **Crop** - a small crop box is **zoomed in** so it is easy to adjust, and the view fits it again after each drag.
- **Alight Motion import** - **Mirror**, **Matte Choker**, **Luma Stamper** and **Glow** look **the same as in Alight Motion**, and **Chromatic Zoom Blur** looks very close. Projects made in AbsentMotion look as before.
- **Poco F6 and other Adreno 735 phones** - start with **OpenGL**, since effects, text and the selection outline drew wrongly with Vulkan.
- **New website** - the app's links point to **absentmotion.com**.
- **Compatible version** - the About page says which features **ask first**, instead of calling them left out.

## Bug fixes
- **Motion Blur** - a layer with Motion Blur and **another effect** was **cut to the bottom right** when playback stopped and in exports.
- **Export screen in Dark mode** - the finished export screen **stayed white**.
- **Selection outline on 3D effects** - the outline stayed flat on layers turned by **Raster Extrude** or **Flip Layer** in 3D.
