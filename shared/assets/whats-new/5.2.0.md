# What's new in 5.2

The **Community library**, **more videos open on phones**, **double-tap to type**, **your own theme colour and font**, and **Alight Motion imports** that look closer to the original.

## New features
- **Community library** - the **Community** tab lists **projects, presets and tools** shared by other people. Swipe between the pages, filter, read the details and **Add** one to your app.
- **More videos open on phones** - **AVI** (DivX and Xvid), **WMV** and videos with **AC-3 sound** that your phone could not play now open, preview and export. So do **10-bit and 4:2:2 H.264**, **ProRes** and **DNxHD** videos.
- **FFamp media reader** - a new reader for videos and sound, **experimental**. The first start asks once whether to try it; change it any time in **Settings > Playback > Media reader**.
- **Double-tap to type** - one tap on a number **selects it** for the ruler or slider; a **double tap** (double-click on a computer) opens typing with the whole value selected.
- **Theme color and App font** - pick an **accent colour**, or your own, and a **font for the whole app** in **Settings > Editor**.
- **Current Frame as...** - saving a picture of the current frame has its **own screen** with size, format and quality.
- **Text Blips voices** - a new **Skeleton** voice, and **Custom Sound** plays any sound file you pick.
- **Keyboard shortcuts on computers** - a **Keyboard shortcuts** page in Settings lists every shortcut and lets you **change any of them**. New shortcuts copy and paste keyframes, jump between keyframes and add bookmarks.
- **Hold a button to see its name** - on phones, **hold an icon button** to see what it does; letting go does not press it.
- **Undo notes** - the note says **what was undone**, like "Undid: Add Blur". **Tap it** to go to that change, **swipe it down** to close it.

## Improvements
- **Alight Motion import** - many more effects look **the same or very close** to Alight Motion: blurs, tiles and patterns, colour and key effects, shadows and glows, warps, wipes and the **easing curves**. Some effects get **new settings** to match, always at the end of their list.
- **Landscape tablets and computers** - pages and tools **use the width**: New project, Settings, Export and the tools open as wide cards instead of a phone-shaped frame.
- **AI Smooth export** - the screen says **Downloading AI Smooth** while the model downloads, then shows the export, and the **time left** is steadier.
- **Export on computers** - **right-click** a finished export's preview to **copy the file**, and the time estimate says **Calculating** until it knows.
- **Timeline on computers** - a selection box **keeps its corner** while the timeline scrolls, and holding the pointer at an edge scrolls it.
- **New project on phones** - a big **Create project** button, like on tablets.
- **Translations** - everything new is translated into all **24 languages**.

## Bug fixes
- **Spaces in text** - many spaces in a row ran **past the text box**; now they wrap to the next line and push the text down.
- **End of playback** - the playhead stopped **one frame before the end**.
- **Frame Rate on Copy Background** - the copied background now holds for each step, so effects after it work again.
- **Exports on phones** - an encoder that stopped in the background showed an error; the export now **tries another encoder**.
- **Sound in camera videos** - **24-bit sound** that the phone could not play was missing.
- **Export window on computers** - it could not be closed with **X** or a click outside after the export finished.
- **Colour wheel on Windows** - the bottom of the **degrees** was cut off.
