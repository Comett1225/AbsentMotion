# What's new in 5.2.3

**Import projects from a link**, **added and Community effects**, **Recommended effects**, a clearer **Group** button, **drag to reorder Tools**, and fixes for **preview audio**, **effect colors** and **Vignette**.

## New features
- **Import project from link** - paste a link to a **project file** or a public **Alight Motion share**. You see its **title and picture** before you import.
- **Added effects** - add effects other people made: open **Effects > Added > + Add effect** and pick an **.abmfx** pack or **.json** effect. You see a **preview** and whether it **works on your device** first. Effects sit in **one folder per creator** with an **added** badge; hold one to **show it in its category**, or hold a creator folder to **show it as its own category**.
- **Community effects** - the **Community** tab has a new **Effects** page next to Projects and Presets.
- **Recommended effects** - the **Effects** browser starts with **three effects picked for the selected layer**. It spots a **green screen** (Chroma Key), **beat marks** (Beat Shake), **quick clips** (Shake), **cut-out pictures** (Drop Shadow) and **dark footage** (Exposure).

## Improvements
- **Change layout in Tools** - **hold a tool** and drag it **anywhere**, all the way to the top or bottom, or keep using the **arrows**.
- **Grouping** - selecting several layers shows a labeled **Group** button. **Group & Mask** and **Group & Exclude** are under **Group options**.
- **Alight Motion import** keeps more settings in older projects, including **Oscillate**, **Move Along Path**, **360° Reorient Sphere** and **Blur**.
- **Effect colors** - tap a color to open the **full color picker**. The **palette button** has the **eyedropper**.

## Bug fixes
- **Preview audio** stops right away when paused and stays in sync when the device is busy.
- **Vignette** keeps its shading on screen when you zoom in on a layer that fills the frame.
- **Settings menu** - tapping the **dark area** beside it only **closes the menu**. Projects behind it can't be opened or pressed by accident.
