# What's new in 5.0.2

**Shape options**: the newer shapes have real settings now, so you can round them, resize them and reshape them without moving points.

## New features
- **Round Corners** - every shape can round its corners, also shapes you drew or edited point by point.
- **Shape options** - Gear teeth, Heart notch and point, Ring thickness and sweep, rays, points, bumps, holes, arrow and outline thickness and more. They can all be **animated**.
- **Phone and Panel** - two new shapes with border, camera, header and grid options, for mock-ups, charts and comic panels.
- **Advanced** - the less common options wait behind **Advanced** at the bottom of Shape Options.
- **Edit Points** - turns a shape with options into points when you want to move them by hand. Undo brings the options back.
- **Edit Points and Pen Tool** - now open inside the editor: the top bar, the play bar and the timeline stay, only the bottom panel changes, and the preview keeps its size. A point feels a little heavier when you drag it, and the other points hide while you move one.
- **Selecting several layers** - the bar now has **Trim start**, **Split** and **Trim end**, and the effects actions moved into **More**.

## Bug fixes
- **Pinching several layers** - the layers follow your fingers and stay where they are when you let go.
- **Transform on the desktop app** - Move, Rotate, Size, Tilt, Skew and Pivot all show again; only Rotate was visible.
- **Tilted layers** - the selection frame no longer stretches into a long band when a layer is tilted a lot, and a tilted layer cannot be selected by tapping beyond its edge. Align, Distribute and Group no longer throw such layers far away.
- **Desktop** - cut-off captions and labels in the Transform and Shape Options panels, empty names for unnamed layers, and the Crop rows spread over the whole inspector are fixed.
- **Project files** - a project package no longer lists parts of our own shapes as "unknown", and 3D model files keep names that Windows accepts.
