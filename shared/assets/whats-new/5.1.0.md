# What's new in 5.1

**Light and Dark mode**, **Time Remap**, **tools inside the editor**, the **Video Downloader on your computer** and **updates that install themselves**.

## New features
- **Light and Dark mode** - pick one on the first start, or later in **Settings > Editor > Theme**. Dark makes the whole app dark, Home included, and switching **fades smoothly** from one to the other.
- **Time Remap** - a new **Remap** tab in **Speed** lets you keyframe which moment of a video shows, to **speed it up**, **freeze it** or **play it backwards** inside one layer. The clip's sound is muted while it is on.
- **Tools inside the editor** - swipe the tabs of the **+** menu to the left to find **Tools**, and **Tools** in the left rail on a computer. Compress, Trim, Extract Audio, Make a GIF and the Video Downloader **add their result straight to your project**.
- **Video Downloader on Windows, Mac and Linux** - the same downloader as on phones, with a one-time add-on.
- **Video Feedback: Frame by Frame** - each copy shows the layer **one frame earlier**, videos included, so movement leaves **trails that swirl into the tunnel**. New Video Feedback effects use it, and older projects look the same as before.
- **Page Curl** - turns the layer **like a page** and shows its back. With **Follow Shape**, a round picture or a 3D model curls from **its own edge**.
- **Scatter Repeat** - **scatters copies** of the layer at random inside a circle.
- **3D Text** - a new tile in the **3D** and **Text** effects turns a text layer into **solid 3D letters** you can turn. It adds Raster Extrude, which works on any layer.
- **Turn 3D effects on the preview** - with the settings of a 3D effect open (3D Text, Cube and the others), **drag on the preview** to turn it in any direction; two fingers twist it.
- **Effect previews** - an effect's page opens with a **big, sharp preview** that fades into its name and description. Moving effect pictures play at a smooth **30 fps** (12 fps on the Compatible build).
- **Smoother menus** - the effects panel and the effect pages **slide in from the right** and back out, and a project **opens with a soft zoom**.
- **Community bundle** - the arrow on **Project Bundle** in Export adds a **title**, a **thumbnail** you pick on your project's timeline with **Choose thumbnail**, a description, tags and your name, ready to **share with the community**. Opening one shows its details. The thumbnail is **square**: scrub smoothly to the moment you want and move or resize the square on the picture.
- **Bundles folder** - pick a folder once in **Settings > Projects and media** or with **Save in** on the bundle page, and every project bundle **saves straight there**.
- **New tablet layout** - in landscape the editor has **tool rails on both sides** and a side panel that **stays in place**, Home has a bottom bar with a round **+**, and New project and Export open in a **centred card** with a big pill button.
- **Simpler Transform** - four tabs on phones and tablets: **double-tap Move** for the pivot, and **3D rotation** is in the **Rotate** menu, with a ruler for each axis or **Free rotate**.
- **Smaller app** - the app download went from about **80 MB to 49 MB**: other languages unpack when you pick them, and **background removal** downloads the first time you use it.
- **Hold to pick** - hold the **three dots** on a project, slide to Export, Delete or any other entry and **let go** to run it. A small **vibration** marks each entry you pass.
- **Bottom bar** - tabs light up with a **soft highlight** when you press them, and you can **hold and slide** along the bar to switch tabs, with a small vibration for each one.
- **3D effects and 3D models** - a 3D model is already 3D, so the **3D** effects (Cube, Box, Raster Extrude and the rest) are greyed out for it.
- **Font filters** - the filter menu in the font picker is **cleaner**: more room between choices, a **Popular / Alphabetical** switch, a proper language menu and an Import Fonts button.
- **Clearer Settings** - an open section is **shaded**, so you can see which settings belong to it.
- **Community tab** - a new tab on Home, next to Projects. Sharing with the community **arrives in 5.2**.
- **Favourites** - a favourite effect or preset shows a **filled star**, with a small pop when you tap it.

## Updates on a computer
- **Updates install themselves** - after the download the app closes, updates and opens again, with **no second click**.
- **Every copy closes** - all open AbsentMotion windows close before the update, so no file is left in use.
- **Windows** - updates go through AbsentMotion's **own installer**, about **12 times faster** than the Windows installer: about **11 seconds** from the click to the new window instead of about **2 minutes 15 seconds**. The first update of an older install also removes its Windows installer entry, so that one takes about a minute.
- **Your files stay** - files you put in AbsentMotion's own folder on Windows are kept through updates and when you remove the app.
- **Files in use** - when another program keeps an AbsentMotion file open, the Windows installer **says which program** it is.
- **No accidental downgrade** - running an older Windows installer over a newer AbsentMotion **asks first**.
- **Mac** - no more dragging to Applications: the app **replaces itself**.
- **When an update fails** - the next start says **what went wrong**, for example when antivirus blocked it.

## Alight Motion import
- **Page Curl, Scatter Repeat and Ring** - projects that use them keep them instead of dropping them. Ring comes in as **Torus**.

## Bug fixes
- **Selected layer** - tapping or clicking the selected layer again **drops its focus**.
- **Parenting** - a child layer stays **attached to its parent** when the parent turns or is resized, also when the two pivots are in different places (limbs of a rig used to slide away). With **Parenting Helper** set to Rotation Locked, children stay in place at the joint and keep their own angle.
- **Fonts on a computer** - fonts with a **space in their name** work again on Windows, Mac and Linux: imported ones can be **used and deleted**, and Google Fonts such as Open Sans no longer turn into Roboto.
- **Text** - while you edit a text box you can **zoom out** of the preview, so a long box is easy to stretch.
- **Tools** - after Crop or another tool on the **second page** of a layer's tools, the tools stay on that page.
- **Tools in the + menu** - closing a tool takes you back to the **Tools tab** of the + menu, and **Back** closes the tool instead of the project.
- **Block Noise and similar effects** - changing the block size changes **each block in place**, like Alight Motion, instead of zooming the whole pattern. Also Block Dissolve, Dissolve, Static, Glitch, Beat Glitch and Datamosh.
- **Frame Interpolation** - **Optical flow** gives sharper in-between frames at every level, and at Medium thin lines no longer **break up into patterns**.
- **Smoothing a Time Remap clip** - a trimmed clip keeps showing the **right moments**, and a clip played **backwards** keeps its motion blur.
- **3D models with effects** - Pixelate, Vignette and other effects no longer **cut off or darken** a 3D model that is turned, for example stood up.
- **Effects** - opening the effects panel no longer **flashes white**.
- **Motion Blur** - fast moves blur into **one smooth streak** instead of separate copies, the blur **trails behind the layer** as in Alight Motion, and the paused preview shows the **real blur**.
