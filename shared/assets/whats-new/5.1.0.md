# What's new in 5.1

**Light and Dark mode**, **Time Remap**, **tools inside the editor**, the **Video Downloader on your computer** and **updates that install themselves**.

## New features
- **Light and Dark mode** - **Select your theme** on the first start, or later in **Settings > Editor**. **System default** follows your phone or computer and switches with it, waiting until an export or other job finishes. Dark makes the whole app dark, Home included, and switching **fades smoothly** from one to the other.
- **Time Remap** - a new **Remap** tab in **Speed** lets you keyframe which moment of a video shows, to **speed it up**, **freeze it** or **play it backwards** inside one layer. The clip's sound is muted while it is on.
- **Tools inside the editor** - swipe the tabs of the **+** menu to the left to find **Tools**, and **Tools** in the left rail on a computer. Compress, Trim, Extract Audio, Make a GIF and the Video Downloader **add their result straight to your project**.
- **Video Downloader on Windows, Mac and Linux** - the same downloader as on phones, with a one-time add-on.
- **Video Feedback: Frame by Frame** - each copy shows the layer **one frame earlier**, videos included, so movement leaves **trails that swirl into the tunnel**. On a video, the copies closest to you stay **sharp**. New Video Feedback effects use it, and older projects look the same as before.
- **Page Curl** - turns the layer **like a page** and shows its back. With **Follow Shape**, a round picture or a 3D model curls from **its own edge**.
- **Scatter Repeat** - **scatters copies** of the layer at random inside a circle.
- **3D Text** - a new tile in the **3D** and **Text** effects turns a text layer into **solid 3D letters** you can turn. It adds Raster Extrude, which works on any layer.
- **Turn 3D effects on the preview** - with the settings of a 3D effect open (3D Text, Cube and the others), **drag on the preview** to turn it in any direction; two fingers twist it.
- **Effect previews** - an effect's page opens with a **big, sharp preview** that fades into its name and description. Moving effect pictures play at a smooth **30 fps** (12 fps on the Compatible build).
- **Smoother menus** - the effects panel and the effect pages **slide in from the right** and back out, and a project **opens with a soft zoom**.
- **Community bundle** - the arrow on **Project Bundle** in Export adds a **title**, a **thumbnail** you pick on your project's timeline with **Choose thumbnail**, a description, tags and your name, ready to **share with the community**. Opening one shows its details. The thumbnail is **square**: scrub smoothly to the moment you want and move or resize the square on the picture.
- **Bundles folder** - pick a folder once in **Settings > Projects and media** or with **Save in** on the bundle page, and every project bundle **saves straight there**.
- **New tablet layout** - in landscape the editor has **tool rails on both sides** and a side panel that **stays in place**, Home has a bottom bar with a round **+**, and New project and Export open in a **centred card** with a big pill button. In **portrait** the rails run full height with a fixed panel under the timeline. **Move & Transform** shows the numbers on top and a **bigger pad, dial or ruler** below, **Media and Audio** are grids, Home shows **one row per project**, and pressed buttons get a **soft rounded highlight**.
- **Simpler Transform** - four tabs on phones and tablets: **double-tap Move** for the pivot, and **3D rotation** is in the **Rotate** menu, with a ruler for each axis or **Free rotate**.
- **Smaller app** - the app download is about **a quarter smaller**, from about **80 MB to 62 MB** (Compatible: 72 MB to 52 MB): other languages unpack when you pick them, and **background removal** downloads the first time you use it.
- **Vibration everywhere** - the app's small vibrations now work even when the system's touch vibration is turned off, as long as Vibration is on in AbsentMotion.
- **Hold to pick** - hold the **three dots** on a project, slide to Export, Delete or any other entry and **let go** to run it. A small **vibration** marks each entry you pass.
- **Bottom bar** - tabs light up with a **soft highlight** when you press them, and you can **hold and slide** along the bar to switch tabs, with a small vibration for each one.
- **3D effects and 3D models** - a 3D model is already 3D, so the **3D** effects (Cube, Box, Raster Extrude and the rest) are greyed out for it.
- **Font filters** - the filter menu in the font picker is **cleaner**: more room between choices, a **Popular / Alphabetical** switch, a proper language menu and an Import Fonts button.
- **Clearer Settings** - an open section is **shaded**, so you can see which settings belong to it.
- **Community tab** - a new tab on Home, next to Projects. Sharing with the community **arrives in 5.2**.
- **Favourites** - a favourite effect or preset shows a **filled star**, with a small pop when you tap it.

## Updates on a computer
- **Updates install themselves** - after the download the app closes, updates and opens again, with **no second click**.
- **Every copy closes** - all open AbsentMotion windows close before the update, so no file is left in use.
- **Windows** - updates go through AbsentMotion's **own installer**, about **12 times faster** than the Windows installer: about **11 seconds** from the click to the new window instead of about **2 minutes 15 seconds**. The first update of an older install also removes its Windows installer entry, so that one takes about a minute.
- **Your files stay** - files you put in AbsentMotion's own folder on Windows are kept through updates and when you remove the app.
- **Files in use** - when another program keeps an AbsentMotion file open, the Windows installer **says which program** it is.
- **No accidental downgrade** - running an older Windows installer over a newer AbsentMotion **asks first**.
- **Mac** - no more dragging to Applications: the app **replaces itself**.
- **When an update fails** - the next start says **what went wrong**, for example when antivirus blocked it.

## Alight Motion import
- **Page Curl, Scatter Repeat and Ring** - projects that use them keep them instead of dropping them. Ring comes in as **Torus**.

## Faster
- **Exports on a computer** - the next frame is drawn while the graphics card finishes the last one: about **90 to 104 fps** with effects on an M4 Mac, with the same frames.
- **GIF export** - uses **every core**: a 1080x1920 GIF of 75 frames takes about **0.7 s instead of 3.1 s**.
- **Picture sequences** - PNG sequences are about **4.5 times faster** and JPEG about **3 times**.
- **Particles, ASCII, Palette Map and Retro Dither** - keep their fixed pictures on the graphics chip on Android and draw about **12% faster**.
- **Many selected layers** - moving or scaling them is about **3.5 times faster**, and the editor redraws less while a project plays.
- **Opening projects on Android** - faster, with less stutter right after the start.
- **Mac** - the app **starts faster**, like on Windows and Linux.
- **Windows** - PCs without a graphics-card encoder export H.264 and HEVC in **better quality**.
- **Computers** - big photos in the picker, **HDR and 10-bit videos** and videos the graphics card refuses all load faster.
- **Computers without a graphics card** - frame blending, optical flow, motion-blur smoothing and Datamosh **work** instead of failing.

## Bug fixes
- **Pre-render** - pressing the button while it renders **stops it and keeps the frames**, and pressing again **continues from where it stopped**. A finished pre-render turns **off and on** with a press, and a red one **clears with a press** instead of staying red.
- **Export button on tablets** - no more **white rectangle** on the round orange button when you press it.
- **Selected layer** - tapping or clicking the selected layer again **drops its focus**.
- **Parenting** - a child layer stays **attached to its parent** when the parent turns or is resized, also when the two pivots are in different places (limbs of a rig used to slide away). With **Parenting Helper** set to Rotation Locked, children stay in place at the joint and keep their own angle.
- **Fonts on a computer** - fonts with a **space in their name** work again on Windows, Mac and Linux: imported ones can be **used and deleted**, and Google Fonts such as Open Sans no longer turn into Roboto.
- **Text** - while you edit a text box you can **zoom out** of the preview, so a long box is easy to stretch.
- **Tools** - after Crop or another tool on the **second page** of a layer's tools, the tools stay on that page.
- **Tools in the + menu** - closing a tool takes you back to the **Tools tab** of the + menu, and **Back** closes the tool instead of the project.
- **Block Noise and similar effects** - changing the block size changes **each block in place**, like Alight Motion, instead of zooming the whole pattern. Also Block Dissolve, Dissolve, Static, Glitch, Beat Glitch and Datamosh.
- **Frame Interpolation** - **Optical flow** gives sharper in-between frames at every level, and at Medium thin lines no longer **break up into patterns**.
- **Smoothing a Time Remap clip** - a trimmed clip keeps showing the **right moments**, and a clip played **backwards** keeps its motion blur.
- **Rigged 3D models** - a rigged GLB model, such as a downloaded character, now comes in **standing up** as in other 3D apps. Models already in your projects stay as they are.
- **3D models with effects** - Pixelate, Vignette and other effects no longer **cut off or darken** a 3D model that is turned, for example stood up.
- **Effects** - opening the effects panel no longer **flashes white**.
- **Motion Blur** - fast moves blur into **one smooth streak** instead of separate copies, the blur **trails behind the layer** as in Alight Motion, and the paused preview shows the **real blur**.
- **Press highlights on phones** - rounded buttons, tabs, switches and chips light up **in their own shape** instead of a white rectangle.
- **Small phones** - every layer panel, the colour picker and the Add menu **fit** on 360 dp screens.
- **Long languages** - German, Russian and other long languages **fit** in the editor's tabs, chips, headers and labels.
- **Dark theme** - the export progress bar, the heat warning, Cancel and the Done seek bar are **readable**, and a few switches and checkboxes are visible again.
- **Easier to see** - the eyedropper ring, the save-colour +, the fog swatch marker, the NEW and AI badges, the active speed chip and the "Still working" overlay.
- **Screen readers** - about a dozen buttons that had no label now **have one**.
- **Motion tracking** - no crash when the point count changes while you hold a tracker box.
- **Saving** - a save that runs out of memory is **kept and retried**, with a warning, instead of being lost.
- **Pre-render** - a resumed render's percentage **carries on**, and turning a partial copy back on is **instant**.
- **Tablets** - selecting several layers no longer blocks the **bottom of the timeline**.
- **Turning 3D effects** - a turn on the preview is undone when the system cancels the gesture.
- **Auto captions** - the font browser shows **Loading font** while a font downloads.
- **Video Downloader** - its add-on card no longer freezes at "Downloading" after switching tabs.
- **Tutorial** - Start and Continue no longer **go dead** after a double tap, and leaving it no longer restarts it at step 1.
- **Effect settings** - Keyframe and Easing no longer start on a **hidden setting**.
- **Community bundle** - with 8 tags the other tags dim, and a typed tag is **kept**.
- **Import** - big bundle thumbnails and huge Adobe Animate (FLA) pictures no longer run out of memory, and running out of memory after reading a project shows **Import Failed** instead of closing the app.
- **Home** - the changelog button **stays in place** when you switch tabs.
- **Japanese and Thai** - the theme step's footer no longer says "language".
- **System default theme** - only followed when you pick it, and it applies **at once**.
- **Status bar on Android** - readable on the **Export screen** and after rotating, split screen or a font size change.
- **Three-button navigation bar** - follows the **app's colours** (Android 8.1 showed white buttons on white).
- **Android 8** - the keyboard no longer **pops up** over the editor when you come back to the app.
- **Android 14 and newer** - the preview and thumbnail caches are **freed** again when memory runs low.
- **Video Feedback on three or more videos** - every layer shows its **trail**.
- **Exports** - one failed export no longer keeps later exports on the **slow encoder** until a restart, and a good file is no longer called wrong when the phone is busy.
- **Damaged projects** - a broken project file is refused instead of **crashing or freezing** the app.
- **Android 8 (Compatible)** - a small memory leak is gone.

## On a computer
- **Linux** - no crash on the **first video frame** on Ubuntu 22.04 and 24.04, Debian 12 and Mint.
- **Auto Captions** - no crash on processors **without AVX2**.
- **Big exports** to slow or external drives no longer fail with "stalled" at the end.
- **Mac webcams** that report 30 fps **start**.
- **Quitting** during Compress, Reverse, GIF or a download no longer leaves a **background program running**.
- **Windows installer** - reinstalling over a leftover folder works, an update **never force-closes** a busy app, the app opens again after a failed update, and damaged files and files in use get **clear messages**.
- **Not now** on a ready update no longer says "the update was stopped" next time.
- **Videos** - hardware decoding falls back to software when it has to, a video with an unknown frame rate plays **past its first frame**, and Frame Blending on High is much lighter.
- **Stray .xfl files** open without zipping their whole folder, and a rare **crash at start** is gone.
