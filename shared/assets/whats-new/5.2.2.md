# What's new in 5.2.2

**Updates download in the background**, **share clips to AbsentMotion**, **use a USB drive or folder**, **change the order of your tools**, a **smoother search bar**, and small fixes in **New project**, the **Add menu**, the **Community bundle** and **Export**.

## New features
- **Background updates** - you can **leave AbsentMotion** while an update downloads. A **notification** shows the progress and tells you when the update is **ready to install**. Press **Hide** to close the downloading screen without stopping it.
- **Change layout in Tools** - tap the **⋮ button** at the top right of **Tools** and choose **Change layout** to move tools **up or down**, for example to put **Video Downloader** first.

- **Share to AbsentMotion** - share a video, picture or sound from another app, like **My Files**. In a project it's **added on top**; otherwise choose **a new project**, **one of your projects** or **Cancel**.
- **USB drives and folders** - tap **Drive or folder** in the **Media** tab to add a **USB drive**, **SD card** or any folder. Its clips show in the Media and Audio tabs.

## Improvements
- **Timeline padlock** - the padlock shows only **while clips are selected**, so the left side of the timeline is easier to tap. Locked layers keep a **small padlock** on their thumbnail.
- **New project** - **tap the dark area** above the sheet to close it.
- **Add menu** - a **light shadow** at the edge of the tab row shows when there are **more tabs** to scroll to.
- **Search** - the search bar on **Projects** and **Templates** opens and closes **more smoothly**.

## Bug fixes
- **Community bundle** - the field you're typing in now **stays above the keyboard**.
- **Export** - tapping **Current Frame as...** **selects it** instead of opening its page; the **arrow** opens the page, like Video.
- An **import error** no longer reappears every time the app is reopened.
