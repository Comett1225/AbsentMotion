Liquid Glass

Distort · Added in version 4.4.0

Turns the layer's shape into clear, rounded glass like a phone button: the layers behind bend most near the rim, with a bright edge on the lit side and a softer one opposite. Frost blurs what is behind it and Rainbow Edge splits the colors at the rim.
