Random Displacement

Move · Added in version 3.0.0

Automatically moves the layer smoothly but randomly over time. Seed picks a different movement, Speed sets how fast it wanders and Evolution shifts the pattern.

Random Displacement moves the layer around smoothly and randomly, like a drifting camera. Magnitude is how far it wanders, Speed how fast, and Seed picks a different path. Unlike Auto-Shake the movement is slow and flowing rather than jumpy.
