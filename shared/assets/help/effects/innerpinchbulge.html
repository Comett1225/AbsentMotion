Inner Pinch/Bulge

Distort · Added in version 3.0.0

Bulges the layer out or pinches it in inside a circle of Radius around Center, while the edges of the layer stay where they are. Feather is how far in from the edges the layer starts to move; Gaussian keeps the middle evenly magnified with a shorter falloff.
