Text Spacing

Text · Added in version 5.3.2

Changes the space between the letters and between the lines of a text layer. Letter Spacing spreads the letters apart, or pulls them closer together below 0%. Line Spacing sets how far apart the lines are: 100% is normal and 200% doubles it. Keyframe either one to animate the spacing.
