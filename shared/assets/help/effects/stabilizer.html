Stabilizer

Move · Added in version 5.4.0

Steadies a shaky video layer. Pick Low, Medium or High and the clip's camera movement is measured once and kept with the project; the picture is then moved and turned against the shake, with just enough zoom to hide the edges. Higher levels keep the picture steadier.
