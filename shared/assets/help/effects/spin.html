Spin

Move · Added in version 3.0.0

Turns the layer round and round at a steady speed; below zero it turns the other way.

Spin turns a layer round and round at RPM rotations per minute. 60 is one turn per second; a negative number turns it the other way. Good for loading wheels, fans and records.
