Feather

Key · Added in version 3.0.0

Softens the alpha edges of the layer. Inward fades into the existing outline; Blur Alpha also spreads the softened edge outside it.

Feather softens the edge of the layer. Strength is how wide the soft edge is. Edges is Inward (the edge fades into the layer) or Blur Alpha (the soft edge also spreads outside the original outline). Use it after Chroma Key for a cleaner cut-out.
