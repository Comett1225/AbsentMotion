Exposure

Color · Added in version 2.1.0 or earlier

Simulates photographic exposure with stops, offset, and gamma correction. Brighten See-Through Parts lets partly transparent pixels, such as a blurred edge, brighten past white.

Stops brightens or darkens the picture the way a camera does: one stop doubles or halves the light, and it goes from -4 to 4. Offset lifts or lowers everything evenly, and Gamma changes the midtones.
