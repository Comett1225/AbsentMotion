Palette Map

Color · Added in version 3.0.0

Maps each pixel to the nearest color in a palette: up to eight colors you pick, or a palette of up to 512 colors from a preset or an imported file. Dither patterns mix neighbouring palette colors to show more shades. Alpha blends the mapped colors with the original layer.
