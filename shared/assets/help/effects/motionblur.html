Motion Blur

Blur · Added in version 2.1.0 or earlier

Blurs the layer along the way it moves, turns and grows, from its keyframes and from motion effects such as Auto-Shake and Oscillate. Tune scales the blur. The preview shows a quicker blur unless Full Blur is on.

Motion Blur smears a layer along the way it moves, turns and grows. It reads the movement from the layer's keyframes and from motion effects such as Auto-Shake and Oscillate, so there is nothing to draw by hand. Tune makes the blur stronger or weaker, and the switches Position, Scale and Angle choose which kinds of movement blur.

The editor preview shows a quick approximation. Turn on Full Blur in the preview strip to see the accurate version; exports are always accurate. See The editor screen.
