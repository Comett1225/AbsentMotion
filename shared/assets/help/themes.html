Themes

Make your own theme in the app or as a file, share it, and see what every theme color changes.

A theme changes the colors of the whole app: the editor, the timeline, the home screen and the menus. You can pick a built-in theme, make your own in the app, or load a theme file someone shared.

Pick a theme

Open Settings and use Select your theme: System default, Light, Dark, Daylight, Light Green or Custom (your own theme, once you have one). Theme color under it changes only the accent color on top of the theme. See Settings.

Make a theme in the app

Open Settings, tap Select your theme and choose Create theme…. The page lists every theme color in groups, each with a short note on where it shows and its color code.

  • Import theme file… at the top loads a theme file into the page, for example one someone shared, so you can change it before saving.
  • Start from fills every color from a built-in theme (or your Custom one), so you only change what you want.
  • Tap a color to open the color picker. The whole app changes as you edit, so you can see the color in place.
  • Give the theme a Name, then tap Save theme. It becomes your Custom theme and is applied at once. Saving sets Theme color back to Default, so the theme's own accent shows.
  • Export saves the theme as a .json file you can share or keep.
  • Closing the page without saving puts your old theme back.

Only one Custom theme is kept at a time. Saving or importing a new one replaces it.

Make a theme file

A theme file is a small text file in JSON format that lists the colors you want to change. Save this as mint.json:

{
  "name": "Mint",
  "Accent": "#5BD6A4",
  "HomeAccent": "#1B7A4A"
}

Load it with Settings > Select your theme > Import theme file…. The app looks the same as before, except that the accent is mint green.

  • The file is plain text in JSON format, saved with the .json ending, in UTF-8, and at most 64 KB.
  • name is optional and is cut to 40 characters.
  • Every other line is a color name from the tables below, a colon, and the color in quotes. Names are written exactly as in the tables, with capital letters. Put a comma after every line except the last.
  • Colors are written as "#RRGGBB" (red, green, blue) or "#AARRGGBB" (alpha first). #RGB is not accepted.
  • Alpha is how solid a color is: FF is fully solid, 80 is half see-through and 00 is invisible. Most colors should be solid. Alpha is meant for the veils and press colors: Scrim, MenuScrim, PressEditor and PressHome.
  • Colors you leave out keep the AbsentMotion look. If you set Gold and leave out Favorite, the favorite star takes the Gold color. SelectionBorder and SelectionHandle follow Accent and OnDark the same way.
  • A name that is not in the tables is ignored. The file needs at least one color, and if a color is written the wrong way, the app refuses the file and names the color.

Tips

  • The editor and the home screen are separate. The editor colors are dark in the AbsentMotion look and the home screen colors are light; you can make either one light or dark.
  • Keep text readable: text colors (OnDark, HomeInk and the others starting with On) need to stand out against the colors behind them.
  • Some colors only show in a few places, such as the number keypad, the export preview or a switch that is on. Open those places while you edit to check them.
  • To share a theme, export it from Create theme… and send the .json file. Anyone can load it with Import theme file….

Every color

The colors are in groups. The second column says the main places each one shows; some show in more places.

Editor

NameWhere it shows
TopBarThe editor's main background: the top bar, and the page behind the panels. Also the full-screen tool screens, such as presets.
PreviewBgThe area behind the video preview, the inside of the panels' dark boxes (sliders, pickers), and the empty space after the end of the timeline.
TimelineBgThe timeline background, the play bar under the preview, and the row behind the layer tabs.
PanelBottom sheets and tool panels (voice, captions, stretch), the media library on a computer, and the level meters.
PanelInsetBoxes sitting on a panel: tiles in the element library, the font browser, the number keys' resting color, unselected options.
ButtonButtons and tiles in the editor, effect rows, and the line between the timeline's layer names and the bars.
FieldDialogs in the editor, text fields, list rows and menu backgrounds.
FieldHiSelected or raised items: the chosen tab in a switch, sliders' empty track, the edge of the project safety dialogs, and some buttons on the home screen of a computer.
ChosenThe graph background in the Easing panel, the picked item in Border and Shadow, and the dark end of the gradient preview.
WellDark wells: tiles in the Media and Audio tabs, slider tracks (Blending, switches), and the Done key on the number keypad.
DeepestThe darkest areas: the underline on the top bar, the color picker grid, the level meter background and the text editor's top strip.
LineThin dividing lines: timeline ticks and header edge, list lines in menus, borders of the tutorial card and the font browser.
MenuLineDivider lines inside the editor menus and the Easing panel's menu.
PressEditorThe press and hover highlight on the computer editor.

Timeline

These are the layer bars and marks in the timeline.

NameWhere it shows
LayerBarThe bar of a normal layer on the timeline (video, picture, shape and so on).
LayerBarStripeThe stripes drawn on top of a layer bar.
TextBarThe bar of a text layer on the timeline.
TextBarStripeThe stripes drawn on a text layer bar.
AudioBarThe bar of a sound layer on the timeline.
AudioWaveThe sound wave drawn inside a sound bar.
OnBarThe layer name written on layer and text bars.
OnAudioBarThe layer name written on a sound bar.
BookmarkThe bookmark markers on the timeline.
GoldRetime marks on the timeline, the unselected points in the preview, the search text and the progress ring in the font browser, the fine-adjust line in sliders, and the numbers on the keypad that are outside the range.
SelectionBorderThe border around the selected clip on the timeline on a computer. If you leave it out, it follows Accent.
SelectionHandleThe trim handles at both ends of the selected clip on the timeline on a computer. If you leave it out, it follows OnDark.

Keypad and menus

The number keypad in the layer tools, and the dark boxes behind previews.

NameWhere it shows
KeyDigitThe number keys (0 to 9) of the number keypad in the layer tools.
KeySymbolThe sign, point and backspace keys of the same keypad.
CoolThe blue label on a keypad key, and the outline of the incoming side in the motion sheets.
CoolDeepThe background behind the selected number on a keypad key.
ScrimThe dark boxes behind previews: tool previews on the home screen (range, compress, tool kit), export preview and the finished export screen, and Community thumbnails that have no picture.
OnScrimText and icons on top of the Scrim: export preview buttons, the page count, the play button, and the placeholder icon on Community items.

Accent

NameWhere it shows
AccentThe main color. Selected items, active sliders, the Export button on the editor top bar, the snap line and selection on the timeline, the play marker and the main buttons. Used in the most places of all.
AccentLitA brighter accent: volume and blending sliders, the thumb of the editor's switches, the folder icon on the computer editor.
AccentGlowA light accent used for highlights inside panels, such as the border and label of the active keypad key.
AccentDeepA dark accent: the track of an editor switch when it is on, the preview's handles, and the ring of a chosen color.
OnAccentText and icons on top of the accent: the Export icon, accent buttons, chosen chips.
WarningWarnings and errors: outdated preview, missing files, limits that the clip does not fit, and the delete or danger button in the project safety dialogs.
FavoriteThe filled star of favorites in the text editor and the effect browser.

Text on dark

Text and icons in the editor.

NameWhere it shows
OnDarkThe main text and icons in the editor. The most used color of all.
OnDarkDimSofter text and icons: counts, hints, empty-list messages, small icons on the computer editor.
CaptionSmall help text and arrows in panels, and the guide lines in the point editor.
FaintQuiet icons and lines: unselected icons in the point editor, Blending and Border and Shadow icons, tick marks, the color picker's idle state.
InactiveSwitched-off or idle things: the timeline ruler ticks and grips, switch tracks that are off, and the Link and Camera boxes in the move panels.

Home screen

The home screen, the settings drawer, the New project sheet, the Export screen and the onboarding screens.

NameWhere it shows
HomeBgThe home screen background, and the soft boxes on the home screen, such as the What's new header and text fields in dialogs.
CardCards and rows on the home screen: Tools, Templates, the settings drawer's rows, menus, the Export screen top bar and the tutorial card.
SheetBgThe background of sheets: the New project sheet, the Export screen, the onboarding screens and the About screen.
HomeDrawerThe settings drawer's background (phones and tablets).
HomePanelQuiet panels on the home screen: the settings drawer's grouped rows, Community items without a picture, and the safety dialogs' buttons.

OnNavyText and icons on top of Navy, for example on a chosen tab or button.
OnNavyAccentAccent text and icons on Navy: the labels of chosen options, the Export button label, and the progress heat text on the Export screen when the home screen is light.
NavyHiThe chosen row in the settings drawer's lists.
NavyLineLines and borders in the settings drawer's lists, the New project sheet's lists and the Templates tab's chips.
HomeAccentThe accent of the home screen: switches that are on, radio buttons, check marks in menus, alert text on the Export screen, and the danger button in the home safety dialogs.
HomeInkThe main text on the home screen.
HomeMidSecond-level text and icons on the home screen: version line, arrows, handles and labels.
HomeDimThird-level text on the home screen: help lines, tool descriptions and small chevrons.
HomeFaintThe faintest home elements: the unselected page dots and the link icon in the New project sheet.
HomeLineThin lines and borders on the home screen, the track of progress bars, and the chosen range button in tools.
MenuScrimThe veil that covers the home screen when a menu is open (phones and tablets).
PressHomeThe press and hover highlight on the home screen.

Switches and others

NameWhere it shows
SwitchOffThe track of a switch that is off: the settings drawer and the Export screen.
ThumbThe round thumb of a switch that is off, on the Export screen and on the editor's switches, and the marker on its sliders.
