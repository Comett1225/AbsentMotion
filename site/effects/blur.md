**Strength** is the size of the blur: 0 is sharp, and higher numbers soften the layer more. It works on photos, video, shapes, text and groups.

- Animate Strength from a high number down to 0 to bring a title or photo into focus.
- To blur everything that is behind a layer, add an [Adjustment Layer](@effect/backdrop) first and Blur after it.
- To blur along movement, use [Motion Blur](@effect/motionblur). To keep the outline sharp, use [Inner Blur](@effect/innerblur).
