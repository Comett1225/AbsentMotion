Glow draws a halo around the outside of the layer's shape. Set **Radius** for how far it reaches, **Color** for its colour and **Alpha** for how strong it is. **Hardness** changes the halo from a soft fade (low) to a solid outline (high). **Blending Mode** is Screen by default, which lights the area around the shape; Normal paints the colour as it is.

- Animate Radius up and down for a pulsing glow.
- For a glow that comes from the bright parts of a picture instead of from its shape, use [Light Glow](@effect/lightglow) or [Soft Glow](@effect/softglow).
