Font Weight makes the letters of a text layer thinner or bolder. **Weight** goes from 1 to 1000: 100 is thin, 400 regular, 700 bold and 900 black. Animate it with keyframes and the letters grow bolder or thinner smoothly.

It needs a variable font, a font that holds every weight in one file. In the font list these are Raleway, Quicksand, Comfortaa, Lora, Bitter, Orbitron, Playfair Display and Dancing Script, and it also works with a variable font you import. Fonts with fixed weights keep their own look.
