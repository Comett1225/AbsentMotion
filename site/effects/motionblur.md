Motion Blur smears a layer along the way it moves, turns and grows. It reads the movement from the layer's keyframes and from motion effects such as [Auto-Shake](@effect/autoshake) and [Oscillate](@effect/oscillate), so there is nothing to draw by hand. **Tune** makes the blur stronger or weaker, and the switches **Position**, **Scale** and **Angle** choose which kinds of movement blur.

The editor preview shows a quick approximation. Turn on **Full Blur** in the preview strip to see the accurate version; exports are always accurate. See [The editor screen](@editor).
