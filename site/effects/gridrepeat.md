Grid Repeat makes copies of the layer in a grid. **Count** is how many copies, and **Position** sets the spacing between them. The **Range** settings let each copy differ a little from the one before: smaller or larger, turned, more transparent. **Random Order** and **Seed** shuffle which copy gets which values. **Delay per copy** plays each copy's animation a little later than the one before, and **Random Position**, **Random Rotation**, **Random Scale** and **Random Opacity** make every copy a little different. See [Using effects](@effects-guide).
