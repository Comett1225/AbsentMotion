Parenting Helper goes on a child layer, a layer that follows a parent (the parent button in the top bar of the editor), and changes how it follows.

- **Scale** and **Rotation**: **Normal** follows the parent fully, **Locked** keeps the child's own size or angle whatever the parent does, and **Weighted** follows by the **Scale Weight** or **Rotation Weight** you set (100 % is fully, 50 % half, a negative value the other way).
- **Auto Rotate** turns the layer as it moves along the **X Axis** or the **Y Axis**, like a wheel rolling along the ground. **Radius Adjust** makes the wheel bigger or smaller, so it turns slower or faster.
