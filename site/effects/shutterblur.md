True Motion Blur works like a camera shutter. **Shutter Angle** is how long the shutter stays open: 180 degrees is the usual film look and 360 gives the longest trails. **Samples** is how many copies are blended: more is smoother but slower.

It follows the real movement of the layer, including motion effects. The preview shows a quick approximation unless **Full Blur** is on in the preview strip.
