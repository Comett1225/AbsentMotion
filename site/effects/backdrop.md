An Adjustment Layer copies everything drawn below it, so that the effects placed after it change all of those layers at once. Put the layer above the ones you want to grade, add this effect, then add effects such as [Hue & Saturation](@effect/huesaturation) or [Blur](@effect/blur) after it. **Mask To Layer** limits the change to the layer's own shape, which lets you blur or tint only a part of the picture. **Mix** brings the layer's own fill back.
