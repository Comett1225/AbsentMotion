Repeat makes copies of the layer, one after the other. **Count** is the number of copies, **Offset** how far each copy moves from the previous one, **Angle** and **Scale** how each turns and grows, and **Time** delays each copy so they follow the layer like a trail. For other arrangements see [Linear Repeat](@effect/linearrepeat), [Radial Repeat](@effect/radialrepeat) and [Grid Repeat](@effect/gridrepeat).
