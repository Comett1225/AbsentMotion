Text Progress shows a text layer a little at a time. Animate **End** from 0 % to 100 % and the text appears by **Based On**: character, word or line, in the **Direction** you choose. **Appear As** sets how each piece comes in: pop, fade, slide, grow, shrink or blur. The ready-made presets Typewriter, Fade In by Word, Slide Up by Letter and Pop In already have the keyframes set up.
