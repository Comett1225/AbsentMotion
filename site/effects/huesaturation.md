**Hue** turns every color around the color wheel (a full turn is 360 degrees). **Saturation** makes colors stronger or weaker: lower it toward 0 to drain the color, raise it for a bolder look. **Vibrance** boosts the duller colors most, and **Lightness** lightens or darkens the picture.

Animate Hue for a rainbow color cycle. For a black and white look, bring Saturation down to 0.
