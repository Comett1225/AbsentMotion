**Hue** turns every colour around the colour wheel (a full turn is 360 degrees). **Saturation** makes colours stronger or weaker: lower it toward 0 to drain the colour, raise it for a bolder look. **Vibrance** boosts the duller colours most, and **Lightness** lightens or darkens the picture.

Animate Hue for a rainbow colour cycle. For a black and white look, bring Saturation down to 0.
