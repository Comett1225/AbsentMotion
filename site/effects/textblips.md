Text Blips types the text out letter by letter and plays a short blip for each letter, like a character talking in a game. The blips are made by the app itself, and they play with the other sounds of the project, in the preview and in the export.

- **Voice** is Classic, Soft, Deep, High, Robot, Squeak, Typewriter or Chiptune. **Pitch** moves it up or down in semitones, and **Pitch Variation** makes each blip a little different. **Seed** gives another pattern of pitches.
- **Speed** is how many letters are typed per second. **Blip Every** plays a blip on every letter, every second letter and so on.
- Spaces and punctuation are silent. **Punctuation Pause** waits after . , ! ? ; and : before the next letter.
- **Delay** waits before the typing starts. Turn **Type Out** off to show the whole text at once and keep only the sound.
- **Volume** sets how loud the blips are.

The presets Hero, Monster, Robot, Fast Talker and Typewriter are a quick start.
