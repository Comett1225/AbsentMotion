## General notes

- 32-bit budget phones: several 2018 to 2023 entry phones (Android Go editions, Galaxy A0x/A1x, Redmi 9A/9C/10A/12C, Moto E, Moto G Play/G04/G05, other budget models) ship a 32-bit-only Android build even when the chip is 64-bit. They cannot install a 64-bit-only app. They are either not listed or listed with `confidence: low`.
- Vulkan 1.0-only phones: the Snapdragon 4xx/6xx generation with Adreno 504-512 (Galaxy A6/A9 2018, Moto G6/G7, Mi 8 Lite, Redmi Note 7) and PowerVR GE8320 phones launched on Android 8/9 are marked unsupported. Android requires Vulkan 1.1 for 64-bit phones launched on Android 10 or newer, so Android 10+ launches are all marked as having it.
- Android Go editions: always 32-bit and low RAM, not listed.
- Phones launched on Android 9 with Adreno 6xx or Mali-G72/G76 (Pixel 3, Galaxy S10, Mi 9, OnePlus 7) are marked as having Vulkan 1.1 by rule of thumb (GPU generation; the driver on a given unit can differ). Mali-G71 phones (Galaxy A10/A20/A30, M20/M30, A7/A8 2018) are marked `low`.
- The tier follows the last Android version the model received, not the one it launched with.
