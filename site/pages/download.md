## Install in three steps

1. Download the file for your phone: the big button above, or the list of all downloads.
2. Open it from the download notification or the Downloads folder. When Android asks, allow your browser to install apps from this source.
3. Open AbsentMotion. It works offline from the first start.

Later versions install over the old one and keep your projects. More help: [Getting started](@getting-started).

## What you get

- Layers on a timeline: shapes, text, photos, video, audio, drawings, groups and 3D models.
- Keyframes with easing curves for almost every value.
- {{effect_count}} effects, with a [full list](@effects) of every setting.
- Export as MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, GIF, image sequences and audio files.
- Captions, background removal and noise reduction that run on your phone, offline.
- Your projects never leave your phone. There is no account and no tracking: see [Privacy](@privacy).

## What your phone needs

- **Latest version:** Android 13 or newer. **Compatible version:** Android 8 to 12.
- An ARM phone. Almost every phone sold in the last several years is 64-bit; phones with a 32-bit system use the Compatible 32-bit file. Phones without Vulkan 1.1 draw with OpenGL, with a few AI tools left out.
- 4 GB of RAM or more is comfortable. Phones with less can run it but may close the app on large projects.

Not sure? Use the [phone checker](@phone-checker), or read [Requirements and versions](@requirements).

## Check your download

Every release has a `SHA256SUMS.txt` file with the SHA-256 checksum of each APK. After downloading you can compare the checksum of your file with it. The source code and the build instructions are on [GitHub](@repo).
