4.4

4.4 is the last 4.x version. If there is an effect or feature you would like, request it now: from 5.0 on, new features will come more slowly and in smaller numbers than in the 4.x updates.

  • Updated Background Remover: a better image background remover model, with two versions.
  • More effects from CapCut, such as effects that follow the beat.
  • A Particles effect: snow, sparks, confetti or your own layer flying out with gravity, wind and swirl, implemented from the particles in TrueMotion.
  • Updated transition presets, plus new and better ones.
  • Do things to all selected clips at once: paste, remove the audio or add an effect.
  • Other feature requests.

5.0

  • PC support for Windows, Linux, macOS and Chromebook/ChromeOS.
  • Possibly an IPA file for sideloading on iPhones and iPads.
  • No more Play build. Phones with the Play build move to the FOSS build automatically when they update to 5.0; projects and settings stay.
  • During 5.0 and after, there won't be many major patches or features; most updates will focus on stability for the PC version and the new iOS file.

6.x and further

  • Not really planned yet.
