4.3

  • Stronger compatibility with Alight Motion files imported into AbsentMotion.
  • More effects from CapCut.
  • Updated Background Remover: a better image background remover model, with two versions.
  • Borders in Frame & Shadow open and close smoothly, and a new border opens out of the Add Border button.
  • Phones without Vulkan 1.1, or with a broken Vulkan driver, can run AbsentMotion through OpenGL ES 3.0, with a Graphics setting to switch between the two.
  • The Compatible version runs from Android 8, and a new Compatible 32-bit file covers phones with a 32-bit system, such as many Android Go phones.
  • Other feature requests.

5.0

  • PC support for Windows, Linux, macOS and Chromebook/ChromeOS.
  • Possibly an IPA file for sideloading on iPhones and iPads.
  • During 5.0 and after, there won't be many major patches or features; most updates will focus on stability for the PC version and the new iOS file.

6.x and further

  • Not really planned yet.
